Project Overview

OneOffice CRM is a cross-platform customer relationship management app built with Flutter and a Laravel backend, enabling businesses to manage leads, clients, and tasks from anywhere.

I led the development of the OneOffice CRM App end-to-end — designing the mobile architecture, building the lead/client/task management flows, and connecting them in real time to the business portal via the Laravel API. Published on both Google Play and the App Store.

Key Features:

  • Lead & client management
  • Task & follow-up tracking
  • Real-time sync with business portal
  • Secure authentication & role-based access
  • Push notifications
  • Offline-first data handling

Tech Stack:

Flutter Dart Laravel MySQL REST API
